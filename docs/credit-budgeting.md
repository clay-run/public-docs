# Credit budgeting

**Note:** Credit budgeting is currently in open beta and is only available for Enterprise plan workspaces.

Credit budgets give workspace admins the tools to organize credit spend, prevent overspend, and scale Clay across teams with confidence. Admins create budgets and assign them across the workspace, giving everyone clarity on how credits are being allocated and helping organizations maintain governance as Clay adoption grows.

To access credit budgeting, go to `Settings` → `Budgets`.

## Features

### Filter by budget on the homepage

Once budgets are in use, you can filter the workspace homepage by budget. This lets admins quickly see which resources belong to a given budget and how credits are being distributed.

### Bulk-assign budgets

You can assign a budget to multiple resources at once from the homepage. Select the items you want to update and use the resource management menu to bulk-assign a budget — useful when organizing many workbooks at once.

### Email alerts for budget usage

Budgets send email notifications when spending approaches or reaches configured thresholds, so the right people are alerted before overspend happens.

## How credit budgets relate to credit spend limits

Credit budgets are separate from [credit spend limits](/docs/credit-spend-limits-faq). Credit spend limits set a hard cap on the credits a specific workbook or table can consume. Credit budgets add an organizational layer on top — grouping resources under named budgets for broader spend governance and visibility across the team.

Use both together for the strongest credit control: credit spend limits prevent any single workbook from running over, while credit budgets give admins org-wide visibility and accountability across all their workbooks.
