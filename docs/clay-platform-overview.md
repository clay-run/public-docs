# Clay platform overview

Clay is a no-code platform for go-to-market (GTM) teams. It combines data from 100+ providers, AI-powered research, and workflow automation so sales, marketing, and growth teams can find, enrich, and engage prospects without writing code.

## Core capabilities

### Lead management

Build and maintain lead lists by finding companies and people, enriching their profiles with verified data, and running automated outreach — all from a single table. See [Finding companies and people in Clay](finding-companies-and-people-in-clay.md) and [Email Sequencer](email-sequencer.md).

### Data integration

Clay connects to 100+ data providers — including Apollo, ZoomInfo, LinkedIn, HubSpot, Salesforce, and People Data Labs — so you can pull accurate, up-to-date contact and company data directly into your tables. See [Enrichments](enrichments.md) and [Sources](sources.md).

### AI-powered research

Clay's AI features automate research tasks at scale: [Claygent](claygent-builder.md) browses the web and synthesizes information row-by-row, AI prompts generate personalized messaging, and [Sculptor](sculptor.md) builds entire workflows from natural-language descriptions. See [AI in Clay](ai-in-clay.md).

### Workflow automation

Clay's table-based workflow builder lets you combine sources, enrichments, AI, and integrations into repeatable GTM workflows — no coding required. Workflows can run on a schedule, respond to webhooks, or trigger automatically when new data arrives. See [Sources](sources.md) and [Enrichments](enrichments.md).
