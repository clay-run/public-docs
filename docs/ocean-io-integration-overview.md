# Ocean.io integration

> **Deprecation notice:** Ocean.io's lookalike enrichments and sources have been deprecated and are no longer accessible in Clay as of June 2026. [Clay Lookalikes](clay-lookalikes.md) is the recommended replacement — it covers company and people lookalikes natively, with clustering, Sculptor support, and audience-scale inputs.
