# Professional posts

Gather engagement data from professional social media posts — comments, reactions, shares, and a profile's recent activity.

Clay's professional posts enrichments gather and analyze engagement data from professional social media posts. Use them to track comments, reactions, and sharing patterns, and to understand how content performs and resonates with an audience.

## Enriching data with professional post content

1.  While in a Clay table, click `Add enrichment` and search for `Professional posts`.
2.  Under `Integrations`, select one of the options.

### `Action` Enrich professional post

Use this action to enrich a professional post with post content and metadata.

**Inputs**

-   **Post URL**

### `Action` Get comments on a professional post

Use this action to get top comments on a professional post.

**Inputs**

-   **Post URL**
-   **Max comments (Optional):** Specifies how many comments to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 10.

### `Action` Get shares on a professional post

Use this action to get top shares on a professional post. Shares are only available for activity posts.

**Inputs**

-   **Post URL**
-   **Max shares (Optional):** Specifies how many shares to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 10.

### `Action` Get reactions on a professional post

Use this action to get top reactions (like, love, etc.) on a professional post.

**Inputs**

-   **Post URL**
-   **Max reactions (Optional):** Specifies how many reactions to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 50.

### `Action` Get a person's professional post comments

Use this action to get most recent comments from a professional profile.

**Inputs**

-   **Professional URL**
-   **Max comments (Optional):** Specifies how many comments to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 30.

### `Action` Get a person's professional posts and shares

Use this action to get most recent posts and shares from a professional profile.

**Inputs**

-   **Professional URL**
-   **Max posts and shares (Optional):** Specifies how many posts and shares to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 25.

### `Action` Get a person's professional post reactions

Use this action to get most recent reactions (likes, loves, etc.) from a professional profile.

**Inputs**

-   **Professional URL**
-   **Max reactions (Optional):** Specifies how many reactions to return, with a default of 10 and maximum of 30.

### Run settings

-   **Auto-update**
-   **Only run if:** The enrichment will only run if conditions are met.
