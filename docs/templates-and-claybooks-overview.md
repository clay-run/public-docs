# Templates and Claybooks

Understand Clay use cases with Templates and Claybooks.

Whether you're exploring Clay for the first time or looking to streamline your workflows, **Claybooks** and **Templates** are the perfect starting points to help you set up workflows for making the most out of Clay.

## Lead generation and email outreach

Clay provides an end-to-end workflow for building targeted prospect lists and running personalized outbound campaigns:

- **[Find Companies](find-companies.md)** — Search Clay's proprietary dataset by industry, size, location, revenue, and more to build targeted company lists.
- **[Find People](find-people-overview.md)** — Discover contacts and decision makers at those companies using job title, seniority, and other filters.
- **[Message drafting and AI Snippets](message-drafting-overview.md)** — Write personalized message sequences with AI, generating per-lead dynamic copy automatically.
- **[Email Sequencer](email-sequencer.md)** — Send multi-step email campaigns directly from your Clay table, with scheduling, warmup, and deliverability controls.

For step-by-step walkthroughs covering these workflows, explore the Claybooks and Templates below.

### **Claybooks**

Claybooks showcase what's possible with Clay through interactive, step-by-step guides. Each Claybook highlights a specific use case, such as automating QA for support tickets, enabling you to quickly recreate workflows and achieve your goals.

Explore all available Claybooks at [clay.com/claybooks](http://clay.com/claybooks).

### **Templates**

Templates are ready-to-use starting points that help you implement common workflows in Clay. Simply copy a template, input your data, and run the table instantly. Explore all available templates at [clay.com/templates](https://clay.com/templates).
